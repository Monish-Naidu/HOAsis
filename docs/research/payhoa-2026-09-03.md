# PayHOA, what it does well (web research, 2026-09-03)

Gathered to check ExpressHOA against the incumbent. Concrete features only.

Company: founded 2018 (Lexington KY), $27.5M Series A 2024, profitable,
652k+ users, "6,000+ communities". Web app only; no native app (reviewers
cite this gap repeatedly).

## Resident portal
- Pay by ACH ($2.45 flat), card (3.5% + $0.50, no Amex), paper check, or
  lockbox (free PO box; checks scanned and auto-posted by unit).
- Fees hit the association by default; the board can absorb or pass them
  through. Owners see the fee breakdown before confirming.
- Autopay with an owner-set limit. Payment plans built from an invoice,
  installments run through autopay.
- Running ledger with PDF export; multi-unit owners get one login and one
  checkout; a new owner never sees the prior owner's history.
- ARC requests routed to a committee, maintenance requests, custom forms with
  uploads and custom statuses.
- Amenity scheduling: fees, waivers before booking, approval queue,
  cancellation windows, blackout dates.
- Owner self-registration by link, admin approves matches.

## Board
- Accounting: cash or accrual, custom chart of accounts, custom fiscal year,
  Plaid sync (daily), rules for auto-categorising, splits, journal entries,
  monthly reconciliation, AI reconciliation from a PDF statement.
- Payables: emailed invoices land in an inbox, OCR, approval workflow by
  vendor or amount, statuses draft > approved > paid, pay by ACH or mailed
  check.
- 1099-NEC e-file, 1120-H filing, managed bookkeeping as paid add-ons.
- 30+ reports, branded packets with a table of contents.
- Reserves: journal entries only. No reserve module, no percent-funded view.
- Invoicing: recurring templates, bulk by tag, backdated for opening
  balances. Late fees one-time, recurring, or interest, applied off the due
  date, with automatic reminder and past-due emails. Early-pay discount.
- Violations: photos, private notes, owner-visible comments, custom statuses
  (first notice, second, hearing), templates, a fine that becomes an
  invoice, email/text or one-click USPS mail, per-unit history.
- Communications: unlimited email, SMS, voice; scheduled sends; merge fields;
  tag groups; per-owner delivery log. Print and mail via Lob at $1.05 to
  $1.25. One-way only: no inbound email archive.
- Directory: owners, renters, custom fields, mailing addresses, ownership
  transfer, tags, 19 permission modules read/edit.
- Voting: weighted, by person or home, proxies, anonymous, quorum auto-calc,
  reminders, live results. No e-signature.
- Resale packets fulfilled by staff, fee passed to closing.

## Onboarding and pricing
- Sign up > community name and contact > upload an Excel roster template >
  add bank via Plaid > budget > backdated invoices for balances > upload
  docs > one admin completes Stripe KYC.
- 30-day trial, no card, free onboarding specialist, free US support.
- Per-unit tiers from $49/mo (0 to 25 homes) to $249/mo (401 to 500), every
  feature on every tier. No free tier.

## Reviews
Capterra 4.7 from 724 reviews.
- Praise: support, cheap versus a management company, non-technical
  treasurers productive in days, blasts, centralised requests, tags.
- Complaints: Plaid disconnects and lag (most negative reviews), rigid
  reports, transactions hard to edit after posting, fee confusion, dated
  UI, features hard to find, no inbound email, no mobile app, documents
  become a bin at scale, no reserve module.

## UX
- Admin nav is twenty module tabs. Dashboard is action-oriented.
- Owner portal leads with balance and Pay, then autopay, history, requests,
  documents.

## Top ten
1. Fee-transparent payments, board chooses absorb or pass through, autopay.
2. Late-fee engine with automatic reminders.
3. Lockbox: paper checks post themselves.
4. Payables inbox with OCR, approvals, ACH or check out.
5. Unlimited email/SMS/voice and one-click print and mail.
6. Violations with custom statuses, templates, fines that become invoices.
7. Flat tier pricing, everything included, free migration, no contract.
8. Real HOA accounting: cash/accrual, reconciliation, 1099, 1120-H.
9. Voting with weights, proxies, quorum.
10. Amenity booking with fees, waivers, cancellation charges.
