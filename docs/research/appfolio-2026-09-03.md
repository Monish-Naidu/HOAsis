# AppFolio for community associations, what it does well (web research, 2026-09-03)

Gathered to check ExpressHOA against the enterprise product. Concrete
features only. AppFolio publishes no pricing; figures are secondhand.

## Owner portal (web, iOS, Android)
- Pay by eCheck, debit, credit (no Amex), or cash at 7-Eleven and Walmart by
  barcode slip. Fees shown before submit and posted as their own ledger line.
- Fees are the owner's: eCheck $2.49 flat, debit about $9.99, credit about
  2.99%. Widely resented in reviews.
- Autopay: fixed amount or "pay the outstanding balance" with a cap, skip one
  month without cancelling, reminder email two days before the first.
- Full ledger of charges and payments. Multi-unit owners switch units under
  one login.
- Manager-shared document folders with an email when something new lands.
  An insurance tab for uploading proof of the owner's own policy.
- Maintenance requests with photos and statuses. Architectural requests
  under a Compliance tab: upload plans, message the manager in the thread,
  see status changes.
- Read-only calendar. Amenity reservations are marketed but thin.
- Spanish option. App Store 4.8 from 6.1k ratings.

## Manager side
- Same engine as rentals with the words swapped: associations, homeowners,
  dues, account ledger. Rental framing leaks through for HOA users.
- Double-entry fund accounting (operating and reserve), cost centres,
  non-monthly recurring charges.
- Bank integrations with association banks: lockbox, check images,
  auto-reconciliation, Positive Pay. Bank feed auto-matches.
- Smart Bill Entry: invoices forwarded by email, AI extracts vendor, amount
  and property, queued for approval. Vendor portal. 1099-NEC prep from
  vendor records.
- Late fees: automatic, four modes (flat, percent of month, percent of dues,
  percent of total), grace balance, monthly cap, per-owner override.
- Delinquency reports, dues roll, collections outsourced through Axela.
- Board packets pushed to the portal. No QuickBooks.
- Violations from a field app: geotagged photos, offline, community map,
  escalation stages, letter templates, hand-off to the mailing service.
- Architectural requests routed to a committee, threaded, board votes in
  the portal, one record.
- Board members get roles and tenure, committees, board-only documents,
  approvals for invoices and bids, e-signature check signing.
- Bulk email and text with templates, letter templates, surveys, all logged
  on the contact. Mailing service with production, mailed, delivered
  statuses and USPS address verification.
- No native meetings, voting or elections. Reserve studies via a partner.

## Automation and AI
- Realm-X assistant answers portfolio questions, drafts bulk messages,
  generates reports. Unified inbox with suggested replies. Workflow
  automation in beta. AI tiers gated to 1000+ unit customers.

## Onboarding, pricing, target
- 50-unit minimum, about $0.80 to $1.25 per unit per month with a $280
  floor, setup from $400, no trial, 30 to 60 days to implement.
- Built for management companies running dozens of associations. Every
  third-party reviewer says unsuitable for a self-managed or small HOA.

## Reviews
Capterra 4.5 from 1,891.
- Praise: everything in one place, the portal cuts calls, mobile
  violations, stable accounting.
- Complaints: support (AI deflection, days not hours), the eCheck fee
  introduced without notice, complex accounting setup, rigid workflows,
  limited reports, reserve tracking heavier than it should be, disorganised
  document trees, small portfolios overpay.

## UX
- Manager dashboard is draggable sections: move-ins, portfolio summary,
  income versus expense, maintenance, delinquencies, calendar.
- Board members use the owner portal with extra tabs: approvals, check
  signing, board-only documents, ARC voting. View-only financials, not a
  dashboard.

## Top ten
1. One portal that removes calls: pay, ledger, documents, requests, ARC.
2. Autopay with a cap and skip-a-month.
3. Field violations app with escalation and letters.
4. Print and mail with delivery statuses and address verification.
5. Smart Bill Entry, email an invoice into an approval queue.
6. Board approvals and check signing inside the portal.
7. ARC as a threaded, voteable record.
8. Association-bank integrations: lockbox, auto-reconcile, Positive Pay.
9. Configurable automatic late fees with caps and overrides.
10. Fund accounting with corporate books.

Gaps to exploit: no small-community tier, no voting or meetings, payer-side
fees, rental-shaped dashboard, rigid workflows, weak reserve tracking,
opaque pricing, slow support.
