### 9. Violations, simplified

Two screens today. `src/app/board/violations/page.tsx` (174 lines) lists open
notices in a left column with citation resolution and an evidence viewer, and
`src/components/app/report-queue.tsx` (243 lines) sits on the right with what
neighbours reported.

The refusal these encode must survive any redesign, it is a documented
decision in `docs/decisions/enforcement-and-delivery.md`: **a report is an
input to an investigation and never the basis for enforcement.** There is no
button that promotes a complaint. A board member writes what they personally
saw, and the notice rests on that. The reporter's name is held and never shown
to the accused household. `tests/e2e/09-enforcement.spec.ts` and
`tests/unit/violations.test.ts` both guard this.

Target shape: one queue, tabs for "Needs you", "Open" and "Resolved". Each row
expands to what and where and when, the photos, the status, who reported it
(board only), and the actions: "I went and looked", "Nothing in it", "Send
notice", "Mark resolved". The current two-column split is most of the clunk.

**On city and government violations: yes, include them.** A code enforcement
notice against the association or a home is real board work and has a deadline
attached. Add a `source` of `"neighbor" | "board" | "city"`, a filter for it,
and a way to log one that captures the agency, the case number and the
deadline. Keep it visually distinct from neighbour reports, because a city
notice is not hearsay and does not need somebody to go and look.

**`tests/e2e/09-enforcement.spec.ts` is already broken before any of this.** It
navigates to `/board/requests` and expects "Reported by residents", "3 photos"
and "worth checking before this goes further". All of that moved to
`/board/violations` in the 2026-09-01 design. Fix the spec's routes as part of
this item.
