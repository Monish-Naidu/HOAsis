# The pop pass, 2026-09-19

Monish asked for more colour, more life, finished icons, and a landing page
with pop, and said a larger overhaul was fine. This is what changed, why,
and where the ideas came from. The rules it leaves behind are in
`ui-baseline.md`; this file is the reasoning.

## The brief, and the line we held

"Engaging, like a proper tech startup" and "the product stays calm" are not
in tension if the colour is put where the eye should go and nowhere else.
So the pass has two registers:

- **Marketing moves.** Gradients on the headline word and the primary
  button, solid tinted icon tiles, cards that lift and glow, screenshots
  that tilt, chips that land on the hero. Everything the brand doc says
  about motion language, made visible.
- **The product is alive, not loud.** One gradient button per screen, a
  tinted hairline on a stat tile, a soft tile behind every icon, a check
  that pops when a thing is done, rows that arrive one after another. No
  gradient surfaces, no coloured card headers, nothing that competes with
  a number.

## What changed

**Tokens** (`src/app/globals.css`, `src/lib/tokens.ts`, kept in step)

- `--primary` / `--primary-hover` / `--primary-fg` / `--primary-soft`: a
  saturated blue for the one filled action on a screen. It used to be navy
  (`--brand`), which is a structural colour and reads as furniture.
- `--brand-gradient` and the `bg-brand-gradient` utility: the primary blue
  lit from the top left. `--brand-gradient-text` and `text-gradient` for
  one accent word in a headline. `--aurora` and `bg-aurora`: three soft
  radial pools (blue, violet, teal) for a marketing stage.
- Five tints, each as `--tint-{blue,teal,amber,coral,violet}` with `-soft`
  and `-fg`, in both themes. Tints are recognition, not meaning; `ok`,
  `warn`, `danger` still carry meaning and were re-stepped a touch brighter.
- `--glow-primary` (`shadow-glow`) under a primary button on hover, and
  `--glow-tile` (`shadow-glow-tile`, applied by `.lift`) around a marketing
  card the pointer is on.
- Chart series re-stepped brighter in both themes, same fixed order, same
  categorical contract, `chart-other` unchanged.
- Motion: `--ease-pop` and `--dur-press` join the two easings and three
  durations. Tokens for RN in `motion` in `tokens.ts`.

**Primitives** (`src/components/ui/primitives.tsx`)

- `IconTile`: the finished icon. Rounded square, `soft` (pale field,
  saturated glyph) for the product, `solid` (lit gradient, white glyph,
  hairline highlight) for marketing. Five sizes, optional `ring`. Colour
  comes from `data-tint` and one CSS variable, so a new tint is two lines.
- `SuccessMark`: check pops in, draws itself, sends one ring out. Pay
  receipt, setup Finished.
- `Button` `primary` is the gradient with a sheen on hover and a press
  scale; every variant presses. `Badge` gets the five tints and a
  two-layer status dot. `Stat` and finance `StatTile` take `accent`. `Toggle`
  springs and turns primary. `EmptyState` puts its icon on a tile.
- `CountUp` (`src/components/ui/count-up.tsx`): a figure counts to itself
  on first paint through a ref callback, never an effect, and the server
  renders the final string. Money stays integer cents.

**Motion** (`globals.css`)

`.press`, `.shimmer`, `.lift`, `.tilt-stage`/`.tilt`, `.pop-in`,
`.check-draw`, `.ring-pulse`, `.toast-in`, `.toast-drain`, `.float`,
`.land`, `.knob`, `.stagger`. All listed in the reduced-motion block, which
now also pins `.land` and `.reveal` to their end state and hides the pulse.

**Where it landed**

- Landing: aurora behind the headline, a warm glow blended over the lit
  house (the render itself is untouched), two data-backed chips that land
  and float, gradient accent word, `New` pill, gradient CTAs with glow,
  solid tiles in the assurance strip and every feature list, cards that
  lift, framed screenshots that tilt, a tinted price chip, and a closing
  band that carries the brand's bridge line over two pools of colour.
- Pricing: the dial sits on the aurora, the plan band is the gradient, the
  rate is gradient text, figures pop when the dial moves, the savings
  card is teal.
- Board dashboard: Needs-you rows wear the tint of the tab they open with
  a total in a gradient pill; stat tiles have a tinted hairline, tile and
  count-up; quick actions are tinted tiles that lift on hover; the rail's
  selected pill is the gradient.
- Resident: balance counts up, Pay is the gradient, quick actions and
  activity rows on tiles, the live-meeting row pulses, pinned notice has a
  gradient spine; tab bar active state is a soft primary pill.
- Pay flow: `SuccessMark` on the receipt over a wash of aurora, amount
  counts up, selected rail is a solid tile, autopay day is the gradient.
- Toast: springs up, icon pops a beat later, a hairline drains for as long
  as it will stay.
- Community hero: navy scrim with a brand-blue pool low left, larger
  name, location on a frosted chip.

## Inspirations

Linear for crisp surfaces and the idea that a glow is a shadow with a hue,
not a border. Stripe for the lit icon tile and a gradient that means "the
thing to press". Mercury and Ramp for finance screens where colour sits on
the number's frame, not the number. Vercel for restraint in motion: things
enter fast and settle. Notion and Slack for warmth in tints. Duolingo for
the idea of a micro-celebration, reduced to one ring and one check because
the people here are treasurers.

## Deliberately not done

- No confetti, no sound, no bouncing mascots. One pulse.
- No gradient card surfaces inside the product, no coloured card headers.
- The hero render is exactly as shot; everything around it moved.
- `royal` stays as a token for anything still pointing at it, but new
  marketing CTAs use `bg-brand-gradient`.
- Screens owned by the other engineer this week were not touched; the
  report lists the one-line swaps for them.
