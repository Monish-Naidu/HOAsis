# Overnight QA, 2026-09-23

Monish asked for the whole product to be driven as a real HOA would use it:
connect Stripe, pay with test banks and cards, then found a 20-home
association, run it as the board for a month, and check every tile as an
owner. Everything found is listed here; fixed items name the commit.

## What was driven

- Stripe test mode on **Oakview Commons**: Connect onboarding (Accounts v2),
  card payment 4242, hand-typed bank with micro-deposits verified with 32/45,
  saved methods, autopay plan, webhooks accepted for every event. Books
  reconcile: ledger, owner balance and Stripe agree, fee is Stripe's alone.
- **Maple Ridge Estates** founded by Dana Ortiz (president): 10 founding
  questions, 7 plan steps, 20 homes, 4 owners with emails, bank, amenities,
  governing PDFs, budget line, insurance, vendor, photo.
- Board tabs as Dana: Dashboard, Setting up, Finances (Overview,
  Transactions, Collections, Reserves), Homeowners (add owner, opening
  balances), Notices (sent one), Vendors (recorded a $1,500 payment),
  Requests (approved a fence), Reserve Study (component), Communications
  (dues email), Community (post, pin), Meetings (October meeting, action
  item), Voting (ballot open to Oct 7), Settings.
- October dues issued for all 20 homes through the same RPC the new cron
  runs on the 1st.
- Resident tabs as Priya Raman (two associations): dashboard, pay, account
  statement, ballot (voted), meeting (RSVP), forum (reply, like), documents,
  request (submitted, then saw the board's approval), profile (phone).
- Cron endpoints dry-run against prod: assessments, billing sweep, autopay.

## Open, needs a human or a decision

1. **Add a payment method** (the SetupIntent form on the Pay page) did not
   take clicks from the automation, while the pay form next to it did. Needs
   one real click test. If it fails to load it now says so on screen.
2. **Link extras in the Stripe form.** Klarna and "Bank, $5 back" appear
   beside the chosen rail. Stripe dashboard: Settings > Payment methods >
   Link, turn off Link payment methods.
3. **Resend domain.** yourhoasis.com is still unverified, so every email to
   anyone but the account owner is refused. Dues emails, invites and
   autopay receipts all wait on this.
4. **Radar Pro** is on for the Oakview connected account (waived in test).
5. **Officers need a login.** The plan step cannot appoint an owner who has
   not signed in yet. Copy now says so; the deeper fix is officers by home.
6. **Stripe is not connected for Maple Ridge**, by choice, so Priya's pay
   page says the board has not set up online payments. Correct behaviour.
7. Copy and small tiles: "0 emails sent" reads as success when all failed;
   Vendors payment card shows "Approved by" with no name; balance badge says
   "0 days past due" on the due date; empty homes show "Moved in" as the
   unit's creation date; the Setting up page flashed mid-scroll once.
8. Not built: Apple Pay domain registration, refunds, autopay retry after
   a decline.
